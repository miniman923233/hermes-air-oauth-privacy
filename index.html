Hermes Air PA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27 · Personal desktop assistant for Edmond Man

This page describes how the personal desktop app Hermes Air PA (“the App”) uses Google user data. The App runs locally on the owner’s Mac and is not offered as a public product.

1. Who we are

The App is a private Hermes agent profile used only by Edmond Man to read and organize personal Gmail and Google Calendar on his own devices.

2. Google data we access

  • Gmail (read / modify labels and messages as authorized) to summarize inbox items and prepare draft replies for the owner to review.
  • Google Calendar to read events and, when the owner asks, create or update events on calendars he can write.

The App does not request Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, or Contacts unless the owner later expands scopes on purpose.

3. How data is used

  • Data is used only to provide inbox and calendar assistance to the account owner.
  • Suggested email replies are shown to the owner for confirmation. The App does not send mail unless the owner explicitly confirms that specific message.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train public AI models.

4. Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner’s computer under the Hermes profile directory.
  • Mail and calendar content may appear in local logs or chat summaries the owner can see.
  • We do not share Google user data with third parties, except as required to call Google’s own APIs.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens and local caches can be deleted by removing the Hermes Google token files or revoking access at Google Account → Third-party access. After revoke, the App can no longer call Gmail or Calendar until the owner authorizes again.

6. Limited use

The App’s use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: data is not transferred except as necessary to provide or improve user-facing features, for security, or as required by law.
